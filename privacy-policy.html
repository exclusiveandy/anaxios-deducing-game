Privacy Policy

App: Anaxios - Moderator (the “App”)  ·  Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: the App works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server. We do not collect your personal data, and we do not share, sell or transmit it to anyone.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Anaxios - Moderator app, a moderator and party-game helper for Werewolf-style and Impostor-style games, treats your information. It applies only to this App. The App is provided by [Your name or company name] (“we”, “us”, “our”).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not operate a server or a database for this App, and the App does not send any information from your device to us or to anyone else.

The App does let you type or choose some content so the game can work. This content is created by you and is stored only on your own device:

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is kept
Player names you add to the Players LibraryTo pick who is playing and show names during a gameOn your device only
Player and role pictures you choose from your photo libraryTo show pictures on player and role cardsOn your device only (the App stores a reference to the picture; it does not upload it)
Roles you create or edit, and your custom Impostor words and hintsTo customize the gamesOn your device only
Player ratings (points gained from games)To keep a score for each playerOn your device only
Settings such as the music mute choiceTo remember your preferenceOn your device only

The names you enter may be the names of real people (for example your friends). Because this information never leaves your device, we cannot see it.

2. Device permissions

  • Photos / media library: requested only when you choose to upload a picture for a player or a role. The App only uses the picture you select. You can use the App fully without granting this permission.
  • Keep screen awake: during a game the App prevents the screen from turning off. This does not involve any personal data.
  • Audio: the App plays music and sound effects that are bundled inside it. It does not use your microphone.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, calendar, SMS, call logs or advertising identifier.

3. How we use information

Because we do not receive your information, we do not use it for any purpose. The content stored on your device is used only by the App itself to run the games you start.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose any user data to third parties. The App does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.

Standard app-store services (such as Google Play) may collect information about app downloads and installation under their own privacy policies. We do not control that and receive no personal data from it.

5. Data storage, retention and deletion

All App data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by:

  • deleting individual players, roles or custom words inside the App, or
  • clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the App, which removes all of its data.

Since we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted.

6. Data security

Your data is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. Because the App does not transmit data over the internet, there is no data in transit for us to protect. Please keep your device secure and up to date.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience game helper and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact us using the details below.

8. International users

Because no personal data is collected or transferred by us, the App behaves the same everywhere and no cross-border transfer of your data takes place.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change. If a future version of the App collects any data, this policy will be updated before that change is released.
